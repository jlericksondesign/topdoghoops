# Privacy and Compliance Readiness Packet

Draft date: July 27, 2026

These documents are working drafts for Top Dog Hoops privacy, consent, security, retention, and incident response planning. They are not legal advice and should be reviewed by the league/operator and an attorney before launch.

Primary references used:

* FTC Children's Privacy: https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/privacy-security/childrens-privacy
* FTC COPPA Six-Step Compliance Plan: https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/childrens-online-privacy-protection-rule-six-step-compliance-plan-your-business
* FTC COPPA FAQ: https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/complying-coppa-frequently-asked-questions

## Documents

* `privacy-policy-draft.md` - public-facing Privacy Policy draft.
* `terms-of-use-draft.md` - public-facing Terms of Use draft.
* `coppa-parent-consent-flow.md` - parent consent, consent revocation, and deletion request process.
* `data-retention-deletion.md` - data category retention and deletion practices.
* `admin-access-controls.md` - admin access, MFA, and operational security checklist.
* `incident-response-plan.md` - ongoing maintenance and incident response plan.

## Launch Blockers

Before production launch, resolve these placeholders:

* Legal/operator name.
* Privacy contact email.
* Mailing address or alternate contact method, if required by counsel.
* Whether COPPA applies based on final audience, data collection, and attorney review.
* Final retention periods.
* Final admin list and MFA enforcement process.
* Parent request response SLA.
* Incident notification owner and escalation contacts.
